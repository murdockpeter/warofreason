The Library of Congress record identifies Wyld?s publication date as 1 May 1855 and describes differently scaled insets, including Sevastopol. Its Geography and Map Division reuse statement permits free use/reuse unless an item advisory states otherwise. Original historic copyright wording in an 1855 map is not a modern application-code licence.

The detailed sheet is published in **1856**, not 1855: do not infer its publication date from the period of the depicted siege. The British Library scan was published as evidence in Airey?s address; Commons identifies the digitized book/image and public-domain status, including publication before 1931 for the US. Its metadata warns that the scanned illustration was enhanced for machine interpretation and colours/contrast may differ from the original. The downloaded file is preserved as provided.

Map sheets may contain hindsight and printed troop deployments. These are source-map information, not generated campaign units or live intelligence. The two sheets have independent normalized image coordinates, different scales and orientations. The module does not infer geographic registration, distances, road costs, terrain passability or movement limits. Respect the main map versus inset boundaries when placing counters. Transfer the same force to another sheet only through a deliberate ruling.

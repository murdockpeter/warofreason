# Rules provenance

Formation splitting is a player-agreed reorganisation recorded under the existing campaign operational house-rule boundary. Its same-square transfer and planning/commitment constraints are application policy, not published Maurice movement, capacity or command rules. No unit quality/readiness, date, tactical bonus or movement result is inferred from a split. See [MAP_PLAY.md](MAP_PLAY.md).

Milestone F uses only `house_rule_v0.1/aftermath-1` for loss/recovery timing, allowed rebuilding, commander campaign status, manually agreed placement, siege and victory. The supplied plan's Chapter-12-compatible regular-to-conscript treatment is an explicitly selected campaign policy; it is not presented as a complete official succession/loss procedure. Irregular/artillery variants remain explicit, and tactical disruption never carries over automatically. Epic Points are entered from players' own published materials, with no embedded calculation or copied rules. Campaign exhaustion is separately entered, never inferred from Army Morale. Thresholds, supplied investment, relief, deadline fallback and simultaneous-claim outcomes are provisional. See [CAMPAIGN_AFTERMATH.md](CAMPAIGN_AFTERMATH.md) and versioned `src/data/aftermath.v0.1.json` for exact defaults and timing. Strategic effects require separate review and journaled confirmation.

Milestone E's procedures are `house_rule_v0.1/tabletop-1`, documented in [TABLETOP_WORKFLOW.md](TABLETOP_WORKFLOW.md). Scout eligibility/range/expiry/attempt limits, contact disclosure, battle eligibility, entry edges and reinforcement arrangements are provisional. The umpire selects intelligence after recording a die/modifier/reason; there is no official success table. Outcome labels follow the coding plan's Maurice vocabulary, but the result is entered from the played tabletop battle. No tactical outcome, private card effect, loss/recovery, permanent disruption, campaign exhaustion or siege/victory calculation is inferred. Battle briefs contain original roster data and player-entered notes, without supplied card/rules text.

This companion does not automate Maurice tactical play. No published rules prose, cards, art or reference PDFs are distributed.

Module data distinguishes `official_maurice`, `historical_scenario` and `house_rule_v0.1`, with evidence and versioning. Movement, scouting, column limits, recovery, siege and campaign victory proposals are house rules until reviewed; they must never appear as official Maurice mechanics.

Milestones A–B established the shell and persistence. Milestone C seeds only the published scenario force counts/type/quality (Maurice chapter 11, p. 95), preserves Dutch/Anglo-Allied attribution, and cites historical commander names separately. The schematic map, columns/attachments, concealment, opening date/schedule, siege setup and objectives are labelled provisional. Starting records the reviewed initial state; it does not resolve any operational or tactical action. Tactical Army Morale and campaign exhaustion remain separate concepts.

See [CAMPAIGN_SPEC_FONTENOY.md](CAMPAIGN_SPEC_FONTENOY.md) for exact sources, numeric composition and the limits of the geographical research. Private PDFs are excluded from Git and the package; no source map is traced or reproduced.

Milestone D implements only `house_rule_v0.1/operations-1`: configurable 3-point allowance, road 1, off-road 2, river-crossing +1; orthogonal connectivity; player blocks; friendly stacking; hostile contact stopping before the occupied square; sequential faction phases; and the configured day/deadline advance. Defaults are in `src/data/operations.v0.1.json`. Each operational event records the rule ID/values and its before/after state. No printed Maurice movement, scouting, commander bonus, withdrawal, pursuit, combat, siege or victory rule is claimed. Engagement candidates are a pending tabletop handoff, not resolved battles. Original generated map art is decorative and supplies no road, terrain or river data.
